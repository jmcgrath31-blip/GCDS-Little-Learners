🌟 What Does Leveling Up Mean?

Buddy grows stronger and earns prestigious Buddy Ranks as you learn! Each level requires more XP than the last (40 XP for Lv.2, 65 XP for Lv.3, 90 XP for Lv.4, and +25 more XP per level), rewarding steady practice with confetti celebrations and upgraded profile rank badges.

🗺️ How to Use Every Feature in Little Learners

🎓Student Profiles & 🍎 Teacher Portal

Click the 🎓 Student Pill in the top bar to switch students or create a new Student Profile starting with your Fox companion. Teachers can open 🍎 Teacher Portal to monitor class accuracy, spot weak topics, award bonus stars, print certificates, or export a CSV gradebook.

➗Math, Grammar & Science Categories

Practice across 13 topics! Use the topic dropdown in the top-right of any lesson to focus on Addition, Multiplication, PEMDAS, Fractions, Grammar (Parts of Speech, Punctuation, Spelling, Verb Tenses), or Science (Space, Animals, Earth/Weather/Body). Switch between Easy, Medium, and Hard in ⚙️ Levels.

🏛️World Flags & World Landmarks

Under 🌍 Science & World, select World Flags (48 real flags) or World Landmarks & Wonders (24 verified landmark photos with fun facts). Both modes use a non-repeating shuffle deck so you won't see the same flag or landmark again until the full deck rotates!

✏️Math Scratchpad Drawer

Inside ➗ Math, click ✏️ Scratchpad to open a drawing canvas right above your question. Use your mouse or touchscreen to carry numbers, draw number lines, or sketch fractions. Switch between 4 ink colors, Fine/Marker tip, an eraser, grid paper, Auto-Clear, Undo, or Clear!

⚡60-Second Lightning Round & High Scores

Click ⚡ Lightning Round anytime for a 60-second arcade sprint! Build answer streaks to boost your score multiplier from 1x → 2x → 3x → 4x 🔥. Your Top 5 personal high scores are saved automatically on your device.

🐾Buddy Companion Perks (Scaling by Stars!)

Fox is your starter companion with no base abilities, while every Buddy you unlock in 🛍️ Shop grants a passive bonus that grows stronger as its star price increases—from Cat (+2 XP & +15% Lightning pts) and Bunny (+3s Boss timer & +5s Lightning) up to Lion (double first-hit Boss damage) and Unicorn (2 ⭐ 50/50 Hints, +5 XP & +5s Boss timer)!

🏠Buddy's 3D Room Decorator

Open the 🏠 Room tab in the Shop to buy rugs, bookshelves, telescopes, pet beds, aquariums, lamps, plants, and elemental wonders. Click 🚪 Enter Buddy's 3D Room to step inside a 3D rotatable room where you can drag/tilt the camera, toggle Day/Night lighting, swap furniture live, and click objects to interact with Buddy!

🎁Mystery Star Capsule & Rarity Tiers

Spend 15 ⭐ on the Mystery Star Capsule for a full-screen box opening reveal across 5 rarity tiers (Common 55% · Rare 30% · Epic 10% · Legendary 4% · Elemental 1%), or tap 📊 Odds & Prizes to browse every unlockable wearable, room item, and title!

📜My Stats & Printable Certificate

Click 📜 My Stats in the top header anytime to open your Achievement Report Card showing questions solved per subject, session accuracy, Bosses defeated, and Buddy's current rank—complete with a 🖨️ Print Certificate button!

👾Topic Boss Battles & 🎯 Weak Spots

Click 👾 Battle Topic Boss! to face Professor Fraction, Captain Comma, or Astro-Bot 3000 (7 hits to win +30 ⭐ & a Boss Title). Missed questions automatically join your 🎯 Weak Spots practice deck.

🔍Story Detective: Parts of Speech

Read interactive adventure stories, tap each highlighted word (or click 🔊 Read Aloud / ⏹️ Stop Reading), and classify Nouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Adverbs, and Pronouns for 1 ⭐ each.

🃏Flashcards & Custom Quiz Studio

Build custom study decks, flip cards in Study Mode, test yourself in Practice Quiz Mode, or share decks with friends and teachers using Export JSON ⬇️ and Import JSON ⬆️.

🧠Step-by-Step Math Solver & Quests

Use the 5-column calculator keypad in 🧠 Solver for color-coded PEMDAS & algebra steps (automatically locks during unanswered Math questions to keep practice fair). Visit 🏆 Quests daily for 3 rotating missions and your free Daily Check-In Chest!

🦉
Pro Tips & Keyboard Shortcuts:
  • Quick Answer Keys: Press number keys 1–4 to pick an answer in lessons, Boss Battles, and the Lightning Round, and press Enter for the next question.
  • Mini-Game Controls: Press 1–4 to launch a bonus mini-game, or press Esc anytime to exit a modal.
  • Flashcard Navigation: Use the Left / Right Arrow Keys (← / →) to move between cards and Spacebar or Up / Down Arrows (↑ / ↓) to flip!
